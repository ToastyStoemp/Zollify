## 2. Logic not working as expected

### POS / money
- `modules/tax/src/engine/pdf.ts:116-223`, `PaymentsView.vue:140-162`: PDFs and totals hard-code EUR and sum mixed currencies. Group per currency as `ReportsTab` does.
- `modules/tax/src/engine/clusters.ts:44-62,287`: fee-only clusters are "ready" and bookable at 0.00. Export + statement of the same month double-count because they dedupe on different ref fields (`parse.ts:93,121`). Require `totalPay > 0`; dedupe on `(at, amount, terminal)`.
- `modules/consignment/src/views/StatementTab.vue:1215`, `FeeDialog.vue:83`: currency is free text (`chf` creates a new bucket). Use a `<select>` over `statement.totals`.
- `packages/shared/src/restock.ts:74`: an old slow seller with one sale in the window is treated as a new item (age = first sale in window), inflating `perWeek` and flagging it low on Home. Pass the item's first stock date as `sinceOf(key)`.
- `modules/pos/src/receipt.ts:214`: "à" line prints `round2(amount/qty)` which can disagree with the total after spread discounts. Print list unit price, show discount separately.
- `packages/shared/src/money.ts:1` vs `apps/server/src/modules/tax/types.ts:26`: two `round2` with different results (1.005 rounds differently in tax P&L vs books). Delete the tax copy.
- Default currency fallback is `'CHF'` in `PosView.vue:97`/`ItemsTab.vue:29` but `'EUR'` in `PaymentSettings.vue:861`/`LedgerView.vue:24`.

## 5. Responsive / media queries

Breakpoints in use: 420, 520, 560, 640, 720/721, 860, 900/901, 1024. Shell sidebar collapses at 900 (`App.vue`), phone layout at 720 (`styles.css:91`, `SettingsView.vue:89,197` incl. `matchMedia`), ModalShell card/sheet at 640, POS at 860, label-printer at 640 + sticky at 1024.

Concrete breaks:

- 720-860 px: POS keeps `1fr 20rem` two-column grid with a sticky `100vh` cart under the phone shell (`PosView.vue:1313,1263`).
- 640-720 px: phone shell but desktop centred modals (`ModalShell.vue:59`).
- `label-printer/PrintLabelsView.vue:628`: grid minimum 624 px, collapses at 640. Overflows at 901-915 px (with sidebar) and 641-672 px.
- `peppol-be/DocumentEditor.vue:433`: line editor ~36.6 rem wide inside a 34 rem modal; three different collapse thresholds (520/640) in one modal.
- Fixed `1fr 1fr` form grids never collapse: `PosView.vue:1293`, `StatementTab.vue:268`, `FeeDialog.vue:100`, `PlannerTab.vue:557`, `ProgrammeTab.vue:499`, `ReportsTab.vue:352`, `ArtistDiscounts.vue:136`. Two `type=date` inputs clip at 360 px.

- `WelcomeView.vue:370` `.steps` and `migration/ImportView.vue:427` `.counts`: flex with no wrap, overflow at 375 px.
- Touch targets under 44 px on the till (`PosView.vue:1205-1255`: 1.8-2rem buttons, 22 px bundle chips) and in several settings. Shell base is 2.5 rem.

Recommendation: two breakpoints only, 900 (layout/sidebar) and 720 (phone), documented at the top of `styles.css`; `SettingsView` and `ModalShell` move to those; `@media (pointer: coarse) { button, [role=button] { min-height: 2.75rem } }` in tokens.css replaces per-view overrides.

Dark mode: `tokens.css` cascade (explicit `data-theme` beats OS) is correct. Violations: hard-coded hex in `PosView.vue:1286-1311`, `ClusterCard.vue:2026,2057`; undefined tokens in `PlannerTab.vue`; `MyStoresView.vue:333` fallback differs from the token; three server public pages each ship their own dark palette.
